# Build It. Test It. Own It.
## Community participation challenge • November 9–15, 2026 • Draft/Review Only

**Audience:** New AI builders, freelancers, creators, and entrepreneurs. **Goal:** Make a working inquiry-page prototype, inspect the code, test it, and report an honest limitation. **Safety:** Every example uses a fictional business and invented test data; don't post secrets or client information.

### Starter prompt for your AI builder

> Act as a careful front-end developer and a patient teacher. Help me create a one-page landing page for a FICTIONAL business called Electric Bloom Studio. It must explain the audience's problem, describe its service, have accessible navigation, work on a small phone, and contain a local-only inquiry form with name, email, project type and brief. Do not connect a server, analytics, payments, storage or email. Validate inputs in the browser and clearly say when a submission is ONLY a simulation. Do not invent reviews, client results or real prices. Use distinctive vibrant design, semantic HTML, readable contrast and reduced-motion support. Create a concise test plan. Before making changes, explain what files you will edit; after changes, tell me how to verify the results and what is not production-ready.

### Seven sessions

| Date | Skill | Exercise and evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mon Nov 9 | Define the problem | Write one audience, one real pain point, one safe fictional example and one expected form behavior. |
| Tue Nov 10 | Understand the flow | Diagram click CTA → fill fields → client-side validate → simulated confirmation. Identify the human checkpoint before *any* real communications. |
| Wed Nov 11 | Test behavior | On a phone-sized viewport, navigate with keyboard, leave fields blank, test bad email, and try valid fictional details. |
| Thu Nov 12 | Respect costs | Research domain, hosting, email, maintenance and API requirements for your chosen platform; record actual cited prices, or leave blank. The included prototype runs locally at no service cost. |
| Fri Nov 13 | Expand without hallucinations | Add a static 3-question FAQ or explain an FAQ helper using only fictional answers. Verify each statement. |
| Sat Nov 14 | Feedback & debugging | Share a safe screenshot, one observed error, the smallest fix and what you retested. Ask before showcasing another member's work. |
| Sun Nov 15 | Launch readiness | Write what works, what you tested, what remains unverified, privacy risk, and next step. Do not call a local demo a deployed service. |

### Five-minute test sheet

- [ ] The CTA moves to the form.
- [ ] Blank required fields are rejected with helpful error text.
- [ ] A malformed email address is rejected.
- [ ] A too-short brief is rejected.
- [ ] A valid fictional inquiry displays a local-only confirmation.
- [ ] No network request sends personal information; no input is saved.
- [ ] Phone width has no horizontal page overflow.
- [ ] Keyboard focus is visible and FAQ disclosures open.
- [ ] The site never claims real testimonials, clients, or submitted inquiries.
