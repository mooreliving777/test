    Behind the experience

    Three lenses. One consistent story.

    The same underlying illustration is presented in different ways because audiences make different decisions.

    ◇

    Investor lens

    Frames the problem, proposed growth logic, market assumptions, differentiation, milestones and necessary proof.

    ▣

    Lender lens

    Turns to borrower identity, cash flow, repayment coverage, collateral, records and realistic downside risk.

    ✧

    Grant lens

    Focuses on program purpose, qualified beneficiaries, measurable outcomes, budget eligibility and reporting.
